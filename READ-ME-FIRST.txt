GOD'S EYE VIEW - FREE EDITION
=============================

A free-keys edition of "God's Eye View" by Bilawal Sidhu (MIT license).
Not affiliated with or endorsed by the original author.


START (Windows)
---------------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it (for example your Desktop).
2. Double-click   START-HERE-WINDOWS.bat
3. The first time it installs a few things (about 200 MB, a few minutes).
4. Your browser opens the app.  Keep the black window open while you use it.
   Close the black window to stop.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then
"Run anyway".  The file is a plain text script you can open in Notepad.

You need Node.js (free).  If it is missing the script opens the download page
for you.  Get the "LTS" version.


ADD YOUR FREE API KEYS (optional - the app works without them)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click   ADD-API-KEYS.bat   - it opens your key file in Notepad.

Every key has its sign-up link written right above it.  Paste the key after
the "=" sign, save (Ctrl+S), close Notepad, then restart the app.

  1. 3D cities ........ Cesium ion    https://cesium.com/ion
        also add the tiles once:      https://ion.cesium.com/assetdepot
        token:                        https://ion.cesium.com/tokens
  2. Voice ............ Google Gemini https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
  3. AI summary line .. Groq          https://console.groq.com/keys
  4. Live ships ....... AISStream     https://aisstream.io   (then /account)
  5. Wildfires ........ NASA FIRMS    https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/map_key/
  6. Live traffic ..... TomTom        https://my.tomtom.com/keys
  7. More flights ..... OpenSky       https://opensky-network.org/my-opensky/account

You can also click the POWER UP button inside the app: same keys, same links.


USING VOICE
-----------
Click MIC, or hold SPACE, and talk:
   "Take me to Tokyo."
   "Switch to night vision and turn on the flights layer."
   "How many flights are over Texas right now?"
PRIVACY: on Google's free tier, what you say may be used by Google to improve
its products and may be read by human reviewers.  Do not say anything private.

macOS / Linux
-------------
Install Node.js 24 LTS (https://nodejs.org/en/download), then:
  macOS:  double-click   START-HERE-MAC.command
          (first time: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again)
          keys:          ADD-API-KEYS-MAC.command
  Linux:  in a terminal in this folder:   bash START-HERE-MAC-LINUX.sh
          keys:                           bash ADD-API-KEYS.sh
Built and tested on Windows; the macOS / Linux launchers were checked with
bash on Windows, not on a real Mac or Linux machine.


SLOW COMPUTER?
--------------
The app picks a lighter rendering quality by itself on a weak machine.
To force the lightest one, add  ?quality=minimal  to the end of the address
in the browser (?quality=auto goes back to automatic).
